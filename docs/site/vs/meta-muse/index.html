Shaliach vs Meta Muse

Meta launched Muse in September 2026: a personal agent that books, fills forms and checks out for you. Shaliach is for the person who wants that kind of help, but on a machine they own.

Atlas, the short answer: Muse is one personal agent living in a VM that Meta runs, free for most use. Shaliach is a team of agents on your own Mac or server: more setup, but your logins and files never sit on someone else's machine.

Which one fits

Pick Meta Muse if

  • you want a free personal agent on your phone today
  • your tasks are personal errands: travel, forms, shopping
  • you are in the US and do not want to run anything

Pick Shaliach if

  • you want the agents, their browsers and your logins on hardware you own
  • you already pay for a Claude plan and want to use it around the clock
  • you want several agents working as a team, and to watch or take over any of them
  • you want to read the code before you hand it the keys

Questions

Is Shaliach a Meta Muse alternative?

For work, yes. Muse is a personal agent in Meta's cloud. Shaliach is a team of agents for your business on hardware you own, run from your phone.

Does Shaliach run on Android?

Not yet. There is a Mac app, an iPhone app and a web board you can open in any browser.

Where does Muse keep my data?

In Muse Secure VM, which Meta runs (Meta). Shaliach keeps threads and files on your own machine.
